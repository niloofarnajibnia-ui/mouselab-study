Debriefing

Thank you for participating in this research study. In the consent form, we mentioned that the study focuses on individuals' attitudes and characteristics.

In this study, we aim to explore how thinking about the future influences people's information seeking behavior and decision making processes. By completing the writing task, you were guided to think either about the future or the present, and your decision making patterns were then assessed using the decision making tasks.

We apologize for not telling you the purpose of the study beforehand, as revealing it could have affected your thinking and influenced your responses.

You may choose to withdraw the data you provided before debriefing without penalty or loss of benefits to which you are otherwise entitled.

If you would like your data removed from the study, please contact the researchers and provide your participant code so that your responses can be identified and removed.
